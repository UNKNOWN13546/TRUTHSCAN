[ SECTION 4: THE OPERATIONAL PIPELINE (HOW TRUSTSCAN WORKS) ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From raw input to cryptographically verifiable protection in 6 stages:

  [1] CONTENT
      User inputs SMS text, payment screenshot, suspect URL, QR code image,
      university PDF, or examination document.
          ↓
  [2] ANALYSIS
      Gemini 3.8 Flash Core orchestrates multi-engine analysis across VirusTotal,
      DeepfakeBench, ELA heatmaps, and perceptual dHash hashing.
          ↓
  [3] EVIDENCE
      Grounded extracted indicators, exact keyword matches, pixel compression
      anomalies, and security vendor flags are isolated.
          ↓
  [4] VERIFICATION
      Cryptographic Ed25519 signatures, perceptual hash distances, and
      Zero-Knowledge metadata commitments are evaluated.
          ↓
  [5] FINAL REPORT
      Standardized assessment delivers clear Real vs Fake verdict, threat
      severity score (0-100), grounded rationale, and detector limitations.
          ↓
  [6] ACTION
      Concrete, actionable prevention steps (e.g. block sender, refuse UPI PIN,
      report to cybercrime.gov.in) empower the user to stay safe.
